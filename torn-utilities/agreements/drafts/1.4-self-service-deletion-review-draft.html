1. About TU and Torn's rules

Torn Utilities (TU) is an independent third-party tool. Torn City does not operate, endorse or certify TU. A feature being available in TU does not mean Torn staff have approved that feature.

Your use of TU remains subject to Torn's Rules, its scripting rules and the Torn API terms. This agreement does not override those requirements or authorize prohibited conduct. Stop using a feature if Torn staff instruct you to do so.

TU must use keys and information only for the purposes disclosed here and permissions you have given. Material changes to purposes, recipients or access requirements must be disclosed before the changed use begins.

2. API keys, access and required disclosures

TU never asks for your Torn password. Obtain your key directly from Torn and use the least access needed for the features you choose. Registration accepts Public Only or Limited Access keys. Full Access is not required or accepted for registration. Providing a Limited Access key is not permission to retrieve every selection available through that key.

TU verifies the key's access level and account owner with Torn. You should not need to supply a name or player ID separately for verification. TU may retain your verified Torn name and ID as described below. You may remove your identifying information through Settings > API > Delete My TU Data, as described in section 6.

The public Torn API is read-only. TU uses it for information requests. Supported Snatch and Run item-use requests instead use your signed-in Torn website session and Torn's security token. They are website gameplay actions, not writes through the public API.

3. Local storage, TU sessions and confidentiality

Supported userscript managers. Where TU verifies support for synchronous userscript-manager storage, saved keys are kept in that manager's private storage rather than Torn-origin credential fields. TU copies and verifies legacy credentials before removing their origin copies. This is storage isolation, not encryption. Manager exports, sync and backups follow that manager's settings and policies.

PDA and unsupported environments. Where verified private storage is not available, TU uses unencrypted Torn-origin browser storage for saved keys. Other scripts running on Torn pages may be able to read it. This fallback must be identified in the key settings. If a supported private-storage migration or write fails, TU must not silently switch those credentials to origin storage. Existing data may be retained while the failure is resolved.

TU session tokens. A TU token is a credential for TU's service, distinct from a Torn API key or Torn website session. In supported-manager mode, TU session tokens also use verified private storage. Origin authorization and identity caches use nonsensitive markers instead of token copies. PDA and unsupported hosts retain an unencrypted origin-session-storage fallback.

Under the current configuration, TU sessions expire after no more than 24 hours unless revoked earlier; the server's expiry and account restrictions remain authoritative. Expired private client records are removed when read or cleared. TU does not promise a background deletion process for closed-tab or orphaned records, manager backups or sync copies. The backend stores a hash of the TU session token for authentication; expiry does not delete the historical database row automatically.

Page inputs and other caches. A key you type, paste or reveal is present in the page input and may be observable by other page scripts while displayed. Private credential storage does not isolate every setting, exported file or feature-data cache, and cannot protect against a compromised browser, extension, userscript manager or device.

TU and its operators must protect keys and API-derived information, restrict access to authorized purposes, and not sell, publish or repurpose your key or private API data beyond the disclosures and permissions you have given. Acceptance is not permission for unrelated uses.

Do not include keys, TU tokens or Torn security tokens in screenshots, public issues or support messages. Remove saved keys in TU and revoke or replace them in Torn if you no longer want them used or suspect exposure. Removing an individual saved key alone does not delete remote records. Use Delete My TU Data for the account-identity deletion process in section 6. Neither action recalls copies already exported or revokes a key at Torn.

4. Snatch and Run and user actions

Snatch and Run may monitor stock, calculate quantities, update clocks, sound alerts, check inventory and interpret action results automatically. A stock change or alarm does not authorize automatic item use, destruction, purchase or travel.

Pressing the active action button requests the action displayed on it. Direct item use consumes one selected supported item per press. Where a workflow needs another Torn action step, TU displays that step and requires another user press. An additional destruction or purchase transaction requires its own user action. Information-only checks and status updates do not require an extra click or a second TU popup acknowledging the same action.

Inventory destruction requires acceptance of its separate setup warning and selection of the items to destroy. Actual destruction is permanent. TU must not automatically replay an uncertain submitted gameplay action. A delayed message, countdown or response alone does not prove success or failure; check Torn's result before deciding what to do next.

After a confirmed purchase leaves inventory space, TU offers a choice: depart through the step-by-step return flow, or close Snatch and Run and make additional purchases and travel arrangements manually.

These are TU's intended action boundaries, not a claim of Torn approval for all features. A website POST is still a gameplay action. Chat Archiver's history-loading behavior and other modules must separately comply with Torn's scripting rules.

5. Optional reporting and external services

Foreign-stock reporting is optional and off by default. When enabled on a supported visible shop, it sends item identifiers, quantities, prices, country and observation times to TU. Travel feedback may include the projection, observed availability, quantities and relevant times you choose to submit.

The stock report body does not contain your name, player ID or raw API key. However, delivery includes a TU session token, which lets TU verify and associate the request with your account. Providers may also process IP addresses and request metadata. Do not treat these submissions as anonymous.

Pending stock reports may be retained locally and retried while reporting is enabled. Rate-limit or authorization failures pause delivery and leave reports queued. Disabling reporting stops further automatic delivery while disabled; it does not recall previously accepted reports. This reporting retry behavior does not authorize retries of consumptive gameplay actions.

TU uses the following services for the stated purposes:

  • Torn: API information, identity/key verification and user-requested website actions. API terms and Rules.
  • Cloudflare: hosting, request processing, databases and TU service infrastructure. Terms and Privacy policy.
  • GitHub: script distribution, updates and published agreement files. Terms and Privacy statement.
  • YATA: the TU backend may automatically retrieve public travel-stock information to supplement observations and estimates. This is a backend source-data integration; it does not require sending your registration key to YATA. YATA website.

Automatic integrations must remain within the purposes disclosed here. An additional optional service integration must identify its data use and recipients, provide its applicable terms link and obtain opt-in before it is enabled. TU operators must follow applicable provider and source-data conditions.

Providers can process IP addresses, request timing, browser/platform information and operational logs under their policies. Application database behavior does not establish what provider logs or backups retain. TU must not promise log redaction, deletion periods or operator-access controls that have not been verified in the deployed service.

Stock observations, forecasts and market information may be inaccurate, disputed or delayed. Authentication and duplicate/conflict checks do not prove a reported observation is true. Predictions do not guarantee stock, purchase success or profit.

6. Retention, deletion and your choices

You may decline this agreement, disable optional reporting, stop TU modules, remove an individual saved key, delete your identifying information or uninstall TU. These are different actions. Removing a saved key, uninstalling the script or revoking a key at Torn does not, by itself, erase TU's server-held records.

Delete My TU Data

Use Settings > API > Delete My TU Data to remove your personal identifying information from TU. TU displays the scope and asks you to confirm before it submits the deletion request. This is a self-service process. Ownership verification protects other users' information; deleting your own data must not require accepting a new agreement merely to request deletion.

The deletion scope is your selected, verified account. It includes:

  • API keys saved by TU for that account on this installation, including supported private manager storage, fallback storage and legacy credential copies;
  • your player name, Torn player ID, associated faction/account identity fields, TU session and room credentials, and other information identifying you in TU-controlled active client and server storage;
  • identity links in agreement, access, audit, submission, feedback, multiplayer and other TU records, including identifying hashes or pseudonymous links that still allow TU to associate a retained record with you.

TU's registration service processes a raw API key to verify ownership; it does not write raw registration keys to its application database. Deletion removes saved copies and associated identifying records wherever TU controls them. It is not a statement that the server previously stored a raw API key.

This is an identity-data deletion action, not a general reset of all TU data. Non-identifying preferences, public item/stock observations, aggregate statistics and de-identified service or game records may remain. TU must not call retained information de-identified if a remaining name, player ID, key hash, token or other link still associates it with you. Your request does not delete other players' personal records or change your Torn account, inventory or items.

Confirmation, access removal and returning to TU

TU stops active tools and pending reporting during deletion. It displays a deletion-complete confirmation only after server deletion and the local cleanup have been confirmed. If either part fails or remains uncertain, TU shows that status and a retry/recovery route rather than claiming completion.

Deletion removes your TU access and invalidates your TU sessions, including sessions on other devices. Previously saved acceptance cannot restore access. To use TU again, you must provide an API key again, complete fresh account verification and explicitly accept the current user agreement. TU must not silently restore removed credentials from a legacy cache or reuse an old agreement record. Providing a key again does not automatically re-enable optional reporting or other integrations that require separate consent.

Deleting information from TU does not revoke or delete the API key at Torn. Revoke or replace it in Torn if you want to stop its use by other tools or if you suspect exposure. Do not send credentials or private reports to a public repository issue.

Other copies and provider limits

The local cleanup applies to the TU installation where you perform the action. Other browsers, devices, userscript-manager backups or sync copies can retain local information. Their TU sessions become invalid, but their saved local copies may require separate cleanup on those installations.

Files you exported, screenshots you saved and copies you shared are outside this button's reach. TU cannot recall a recipient's copy. Torn, Cloudflare, GitHub and other providers can retain their own operational logs or historical backups under their policies. Active TU data deletion does not promise immediate erasure from third-party logs, backups or systems TU cannot directly control. TU must not report a broader deletion scope than the implemented process can verify.

7. Responsible use and limitations

Do not use TU to evade Torn's rules, obtain another person's private information without permission, bypass TU authorization or service limits, interfere with other users or extract credentials. Restrictions on TU-hosted service use, redistribution or modification remain subject to applicable permissions, licenses and law, including permitted security research and other protected rights.

TU must minimize requested data, respect Torn's API limits and stop using invalid or disabled keys when the relevant error is received. The Torn API documentation states limits of 100 requests per minute per user across keys and 5,000 per minute per service IP. Torn may change those limits. Additional keys or servers do not authorize bypassing them.

TU's browser and backend safeguards cover their own defined request paths. Other tools and keys on the same account, and other services sharing an outbound IP, may also contribute to the applicable limit. TU does not claim its counters control all such traffic.

TU is provided as-is. Features, third-party services and Torn responses can change or fail. No agreement wording makes a defective or prohibited action compliant. These terms do not limit rights that applicable law does not allow to be waived.

8. Acceptance and changes

By selecting Accept on a published version, you acknowledge the disclosed processing and agree to use TU subject to Torn's rules. TU records acceptance against your verified account and the agreement version. Account-identity deletion removes that account’s identifying acceptance link; returning requires fresh acceptance. Acceptance does not automatically enable optional reporting or another integration requiring separate consent.

A new published agreement version requires renewed acceptance before continued use of features that require it. Materially different key/data uses must not begin under an old acceptance.
