Torn Utilities (TU) is an independent third-party tool. Torn City does not
operate, endorse or certify TU. A feature being available in TU does not mean
Torn staff have approved that feature.

TU must use keys and information only for the purposes disclosed here and
permissions you have given. Material changes to purposes, recipients or access
requirements must be disclosed before the changed use begins.

## 2. API keys, access and required disclosures

TU never asks for your Torn password. Obtain your key directly from Torn and use
the least access needed for the features you choose. Registration accepts
**Public Only or Limited Access** keys. Full Access is not required or accepted
for registration. Providing a Limited Access key is not permission to retrieve
every selection available through that key.

TU verifies the key's access level and account owner with Torn. You should not
need to supply a name or player ID separately for verification. TU may retain
your verified Torn name and ID as described below.

The public Torn API is read-only. TU uses it for information requests. Supported
Snatch and Run item-use requests instead use your signed-in Torn website session
and Torn's security token. They are website gameplay actions, not writes through
the public API.

required access. The same information must be clearly visible at every place

## 3. Local storage, TU sessions and confidentiality

**Supported userscript managers.** Where TU verifies support for synchronous
userscript-manager storage, saved keys are kept in that manager's private
storage rather than Torn-origin credential fields. TU copies and verifies
legacy credentials before removing their origin copies. This is storage
isolation, not encryption. Manager exports, sync and backups follow that
manager's settings and policies.

**PDA and unsupported environments.** Where verified private storage is not
available, TU uses unencrypted Torn-origin browser storage for saved keys.
Other scripts running on Torn pages may be able to read it. This fallback must
be identified in the key settings. If a supported private-storage migration or
write fails, TU must not silently switch those credentials to origin storage.
Existing data may be retained while the failure is resolved.

**TU session tokens.** A TU token is a credential for TU's service, distinct from
a Torn API key or Torn website session. In the prepared supported-manager mode,
TU session tokens also use verified private storage. Origin authorization and
identity caches use nonsensitive markers instead of token copies. PDA and
unsupported hosts retain an unencrypted origin-session-storage fallback.

Under the prepared configuration, TU sessions expire after no more than
24 hours unless revoked earlier; the server's expiry and account restrictions
remain authoritative. Expired private client records are removed when read or
cleared. TU does not promise a background deletion process for closed-tab or
orphaned records, manager backups or sync copies. The backend stores a hash of
the TU session token for authentication; expiry does not delete the historical
database row automatically.

**Page inputs and other caches.** A key you type, paste or reveal is present in
the page input and may be observable by other page scripts while displayed.
Private credential storage does not isolate every setting, exported file or
feature-data cache, and cannot protect against a compromised browser, extension,
userscript manager or device.

TU and its operators must protect keys and API-derived information, restrict
access to authorized purposes, and not sell, publish or repurpose your key or
private API data beyond the disclosures and permissions you have given.
Acceptance is not permission for unrelated uses.

Do not include keys, TU tokens or Torn security tokens in screenshots, public
issues or support messages. Remove saved keys in TU and revoke or replace them
in Torn if you no longer want them used or suspect exposure. Local removal does
not delete records already stored remotely or copies already exported.

## 4. Snatch and Run and user actions

Snatch and Run may monitor stock, calculate quantities, update clocks, sound
alerts, check inventory and interpret action results automatically. A stock
change or alarm does not authorize automatic item use, destruction, purchase
or travel.

Pressing the active action button requests the action displayed on it. Direct
item use consumes one selected supported item per press. Where a workflow needs
another Torn action step, TU displays that step and requires another user press.
An additional destruction or purchase transaction requires its own user action.
Information-only checks and status updates do not require an extra click or a
second TU popup acknowledging the same action.

Inventory destruction requires acceptance of its separate setup warning and
selection of the items to destroy. Actual destruction is permanent. TU must not
automatically replay an uncertain submitted gameplay action. A delayed message,
countdown or response alone does not prove success or failure; check Torn's
result before deciding what to do next.

After a confirmed purchase leaves inventory space, TU offers a choice: depart
through the step-by-step return flow, or close Snatch and Run and make additional
purchases and travel arrangements manually.

These are TU's intended action boundaries, not a claim of Torn approval for all
features. A website POST is still a gameplay action. Chat Archiver's
history-loading behavior and other modules must separately comply with Torn's
scripting rules.

## 5. Optional reporting and external services

Foreign-stock reporting is optional and off by default. When enabled on a
supported visible shop, it sends item identifiers, quantities, prices, country
and observation times to TU. Travel feedback may include the projection,
observed availability, quantities and relevant times you choose to submit.

The stock report body does not contain your name, player ID or raw API key.
However, delivery includes a TU session token, which lets TU verify and associate
the request with your account. Providers may also process IP addresses and
request metadata. Do not treat these submissions as anonymous.

Pending stock reports may be retained locally and retried while reporting is
enabled. Rate-limit or authorization failures pause delivery and leave reports
queued. Disabling reporting stops further automatic delivery while disabled;
it does not recall previously accepted reports. This reporting retry behavior
does not authorize retries of consumptive gameplay actions.

TU uses the following services for the stated purposes:

- **Torn:** API information, identity/key verification and user-requested website
  actions. [API terms](https://www.torn.com/api.html) and
  [Rules](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Rules).
- **Cloudflare:** hosting, request processing, databases and TU service
  infrastructure. [Terms](https://www.cloudflare.com/terms/) and
  [Privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
- **GitHub:** script distribution, updates and published agreement files.
  [Terms](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-of-service)
  and [Privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **YATA:** the TU backend may automatically retrieve public travel-stock
  information to supplement observations and estimates. This is a backend
  source-data integration; it does not require sending your registration key
  to YATA. [YATA website](https://yata.yt/).

Automatic integrations must remain within the purposes disclosed here. An
additional optional service integration must identify its data use and
recipients, provide its applicable terms link and obtain opt-in before it is
enabled. TU operators must follow applicable provider and source-data conditions.

Providers can process IP addresses, request timing, browser/platform information
and operational logs under their policies. Application database behavior does
not establish what provider logs or backups retain. TU must not promise log
redaction, deletion periods or operator-access controls that have not been
verified in the deployed service.

Stock observations, forecasts and market information may be inaccurate, disputed
or delayed. Authentication and duplicate/conflict checks do not prove a reported
observation is true. Predictions do not guarantee stock, purchase success or
profit.

## 6. Retention, deletion and your choices

You may decline this agreement, disable optional reporting, stop TU modules,
remove stored credentials, clear local TU/browser storage and uninstall TU.
Features requiring verification and agreement acceptance will not operate if
you decline.

Persistent local data may survive closing Torn. Session storage may survive
navigation or browser restoration. Private manager records and exported files
can remain separately. Uninstalling TU does not remove copies you exported or
shared, and TU cannot remove a recipient's copy from that recipient's systems.

Uninstalling TU, clearing browser storage or revoking a Torn key does not
automatically erase remote identity, agreement, audit, game, stock or feedback
records. Unless a retention/deletion period has been expressly specified above,
assume records may remain indefinitely. Access expiry, active counter expiry,
physical deletion and backup retention are different events.

For private data-access, deletion or security requests, contact
**RelaxSweety [4539436] through a private Torn message** using the
[author's Torn profile](https://www.torn.com/profiles.php?XID=4539436).
Do not send credentials or private reports to a public repository issue.

The operator must explain any deletion limits, retained security records and
backup retention when responding to a request. This agreement does not promise
an automatic account-deletion service that has not been implemented or guarantee
that information is removed from third-party backups immediately.

## 7. Responsible use and limitations

Do not use TU to evade Torn's rules, obtain another person's private information
without permission, bypass TU authorization or service limits, interfere with
other users or extract credentials. Restrictions on TU-hosted service use,
redistribution or modification remain subject to applicable permissions,
licenses and law, including permitted security research and other protected
rights.

TU must minimize requested data, respect Torn's API limits and stop using
invalid or disabled keys when the relevant error is received. The Torn API text
supplied for this review states limits of **100 requests per minute per user
across keys** and **5,000 per minute per service IP**. Torn may change those
limits. Additional keys or servers do not authorize bypassing them.

TU's browser and backend safeguards cover their own defined request paths.
Other tools and keys on the same account, and other services sharing an outbound
IP, may also contribute to the applicable limit. TU does not claim its counters
control all such traffic.

TU is provided as-is. Features, third-party services and Torn responses can
change or fail. No agreement wording makes a defective or prohibited action
compliant. These terms do not limit rights that applicable law does not allow
to be waived.
